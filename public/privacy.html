Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026 | Application: desk.bizadoc.com

This Privacy Policy describes how BizAdoc Technologies ("BizAdoc," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal and corporate information when you use the BizaDesk Autonomous IT Helpdesk platform, services, and associated web applications accessible at https://desk.bizadoc.com (the "Service").

Core Commitment: BizAdoc does not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your corporate or personal information to data brokers or advertisers. All data collected is utilized solely to deliver and secure automated IT support, ticket workflows, and customer operations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, telemetry collected automatically, and authentication data provided by trusted third-party providers:

  • Account & Profile Information: When registering, we collect your full name, work email address, password hash, and company name.
  • Google User Data (OAuth): When you authenticate via Google Sign-In, we access your Google account email address, full name, and avatar profile picture to provision your identity. We do not request or access private Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or Calendar scopes.
  • IT Support Tickets & Diagnostic Telemetry: Issues, symptoms, device logs, operating system metrics, and diagnostic questions submitted to the Virtual IT Support assistant.
  • Physical Visit Dispatch Details: For on-site technician service requests, we collect physical address, city, scheduled visit window, and primary contact phone numbers.
  • Billing and Transaction Identifiers: We receive payment verification reference IDs, plan types, and timestamps from our payment gateway (Paystack). We do not store, receive, or process raw credit/debit card numbers.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

BizaDesk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Limited Use: Google user data is accessed solely for user authentication and account creation within the BizaDesk application.
  • No Advertisements: Data obtained via Google OAuth is never used for targeting advertisements, sold to third parties, or used to build surveillance profiles.
  • No Human Access: No humans read your Google account data unless authorized by you for resolving technical support or required by applicable law.

3. How We Use Collected Information

We use information collected through BizaDesk for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To authenticate users and enforce multi-tenant Row-Level Security (RLS) data boundaries.
  • To process, route, and resolve technical support incidents and automated diagnostics.
  • To coordinate live communication channels, including attended remote screen assist, technician hotlines, and WhatsApp support.
  • To manage 30-day workspace trials, subscriptions, and billing invoices via Paystack.
  • To send operational notifications, such as ticket status alerts, password reset links, and mandatory two-factor authentication (2FA) security notices.

4. Data Storage, Security & Multi-Tenant Isolation

BizaDesk is engineered with bank-grade security standards. User and organizational data is hosted on Supabase infrastructure with strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. All network communication requires TLS/HTTPS 1.3 encryption. Administrative accounts require mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (2FA/TOTP).

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share minimal necessary data with vetted infrastructure partners who assist in providing the Service:

  • Supabase: Cloud database, authentication, and security infrastructure.
  • Google LLC: Identity authentication via Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Paystack (Stripe Company): PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processing.
  • Netlify: Application hosting and CDN distribution.
  • Resend / Transactional Mail: Delivery of account verification and SLA ticket alerts.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and ticket history at any time. Administrators may delete their workspace or request data erasure by contacting our compliance desk.
